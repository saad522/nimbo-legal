Nimbo privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Who we are

Nimbo is made by Saad Saissi Hassani. Contact: saadsaissi41@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Almost everything. Your profile, your names and reasons, your reward, your money saved, puffs, cravings and slips, your chats with Nimbo, the audio of calls (processed on the device), the message video and your check-ins are stored on your iPhone only. A shared App Group lets Nimbo's widgets read what they show. In Me you can use "Export my data" and "Delete everything" at any time.

The random installation ID

Nimbo makes a random ID the first time it runs. It is not your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not linked to you. A reinstall, or "Delete everything", makes a new one.

App stats

In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Nimbo asks first: only if you say "Yes, I'll help" does it send app stats, and "Not now" sends nothing. Everywhere else, app stats are on from the start, and you can turn them off at any time in Me → Privacy. Where you are is read once, from your App Store country (or your iPhone's region), the first time Nimbo runs.

With app stats on, Nimbo sends them to PostHog (EU, Frankfurt):

  • the events listed in the app's contract (for example which screen or feature was used);
  • taps, named by the element's identifier, never by the text on it;
  • session replay: a recording of the screens, where private things (your name, answers, chats, money) are masked and never leave the iPhone.

We do not keep IP addresses ("Discard client IP data" is on). You can turn this off at any time in Me → Privacy; replay and taps stop at once. To delete stats already sent under an installation ID, write to saadsaissi41@gmail.com with that ID.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. RevenueCat helps us manage subscriptions: it receives the installation ID, your purchases and "where you heard about Nimbo".

Ad measurement. If you install Nimbo from an ad, we use Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution (AdAttributionKit and SKAdNetwork) so the ad network learns, as a count among many people, that its ad led to an install and roughly how far people got (finished the setup, started a trial, subscribed). Apple decides what is shared and when; nothing identifies you. If you came from an Apple Ads search ad, Apple tells RevenueCat which campaign it was, through Apple's AdServices, linked only to the random installation ID. Nimbo never asks to track you, does not use your advertising identifier and has no ad-network code inside.

On-device model

Chat and calls use Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a server for that.

What we don't do

No ads. No tracking across other companies' apps. No data sold.

Age

Nimbo is for people aged 18 and over.

Wellness, not medical care

Nimbo is a wellness companion. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
