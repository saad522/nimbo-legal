Nimbo support

Nimbo is a little cloud who helps you stop vaping or cut down. If something isn't working, or you have a question, write to saadsaissi41@gmail.com. You'll get a reply from the person who makes Nimbo, usually within two days.

Common questions

How do I cancel Nimbo Plus? On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Nimbo. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends so you aren't charged again.

I bought Nimbo Plus but it's locked. On the Nimbo Plus screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Nimbo.

Where is my data? Your chats, notes, puffs and your recorded message stay on your iPhone. You can erase everything in Nimbo's settings with "Delete everything". See the privacy policy.

I'm struggling right now. Nimbo isn't medical advice. If you're in crisis or in danger, call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline for your country at findahelpline.com.
